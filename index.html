API Data Navigation

Data Playlist

Data Playlist is an interactive chart browser powered by my own deployed Cloudflare Worker API. The app fetches chart data and lets users move through all 50 records with Back and Next controls.

I used JavaScript, async/await, JSON, array state, bounds checks, and DOM updates to keep the chart loaded, display the current track, and prevent navigation errors at either end of the dataset.

The Personal Pulse Principle

Preamble

Pulse exists to unite people through music. I believe music is more than entertainment—it is identity, memory, emotion, and connection. Every decision made within Pulse must honor the artist, respect the listener, and strengthen the bond between them.

Article I — The Pulse Principle

Pulse must never be about what I want.
Pulse must never be about what someone else wants.
Pulse must become whatever each individual user makes it through the music they love.
The platform adapts to the user - not the other way around.
Every product decision should answer one question.

“Does this help the user discover and experience their Pulse?”

Article II — The Artist Principle

Artists are the heartbeat.
Without artists, there is no Pulse.
Every feature should amplify the artist rather than compete with them.

Article III — The Fan Principle

Every fan deserves to feel seen.
Whether someone has followed an artist for twenty years or discovered them five minutes ago, Pulse welcomes them equally.

Article IV — The Music Principle

Music comes first.
Algorithms never outrank emotion.
Popularity never outweighs authenticity.

Article V — The Community Principle

Respect creates connection.
Different tastes are celebrated.
Music divides genres. Pulse unites listeners.

Article VI — The Innovation Principle

Technology exists to disappear.
The best feature is the one that makes the music feel closer.

Article VII — The Integrity Principle

Trust is earned.
No deception.
No manipulation.
No unnecessary noise.
Every decision should make Pulse more worthy of the people who use it.

Closing Oath

Keep your finger on the pulse.
Honor the music.
Honor the artist.
Honor each other.

The Dev's Deed

I understand that my work represents my character.

I expect that my work reflects my integrity.

I will approach every challenge with purposeful precision; intention breeds excellence.

I will build something worth building. Build skills that cannot be taken from me, build people up, and build better than I did yesterday.

I will turn my noise down and my music up. Focus on creativity, curiosity, and connection.

I will remember that life is more than deadlines and deliverables. I will forget life long enough to remember to live.

I will leave everything I touch better than I found it.

I will compete with the person I was yesterday only, and never allow comfort to replace growth or discipline.

I will never settle. Not for anyone. Not for anything.
